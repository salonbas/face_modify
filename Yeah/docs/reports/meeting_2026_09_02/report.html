Historical evidence · non-LFW baseline

Historical PGD Result: Why was it 0.347?

Archived historical artifact: results/archive/historical_single_image/pgdfull_musk_v2
PGD Full · ε = 0.04 · 200 steps · historical self cosine = 0.34716704

Semantics: 0.34716704 is self similarity (B ↔ Bmodified), not the new A ↔ Badv verification metric. It is a historical, non-LFW baseline and is excluded from formal LFW statistics.

The historical input was raw musk1 (667×500); the current inputs are 250×250 LFW funneled samples. The difference is consistent with sample/dataset-dependent attackability, not evidence of PGD implementation drift. It does not identify funneling, alignment, pose, scale, face occupancy, or any one cause. Although 0.347 is above 0.1407 numerically, it must not be retroactively labelled a formal verification failure: no independent A/B pair existed.

Development comparison

Why add MI-FGSM? PGD vs MI-FGSM

MI-FGSM (Momentum Iterative Fast Gradient Sign Method; Dong et al., CVPR 2018) accumulates normalized gradients through momentum, a design intended in part to improve transferability. Conditions were held constant: ε = 0.04, 200 steps, α = 0.0002, random start = false, same five identities, ArcFace surrogate, FaceNet victim, and calibrated thresholds. MI-FGSM additionally uses momentum = 1.0 and per-image L1 gradient normalization.

Interpretation and limits

Observed source / transfer tradeoff

PGD had stronger ArcFace source degradation. MI-FGSM had weaker source degradation, but stronger observed FaceNet representation movement and verification degradation. On these five development identities: PGD 0 / 5 victim crossings; MI-FGSM 1 / 5.

Momentum showed a stronger observed cross-model verification effect on these five development identities.

Supported

  • ✓ 0.4 is not a universal ArcFace threshold.
  • ✓ Model-specific calibration gives materially different thresholds.
  • ✓ PGD crosses ArcFace’s boundary in this five-sample capability check.
  • ✓ PGD affects FaceNet representation but did not cross its boundary here.
  • ✓ MI-FGSM showed stronger observed FaceNet degradation; one sample crossed.

Not yet supported

  • ○ PGD has a 100% white-box ASR.
  • ○ MI-FGSM has a 20% transfer ASR.
  • ○ MI-FGSM is statistically better than PGD.
  • ○ LFW funneling causes stronger attacks.
  • ○ Results generalize to other models or real-world systems.

Meeting aid

60-second progress summary

老師上次問為什麼 threshold 用 0.4。我回去查證後確認，0.4 不是 ArcFace 的 universal official threshold，比較像 InsightFace 的 implementation/reference 值。所以我用 LFW 的 development split 分別校準 ArcFace 和 FaceNet，得到 FAR 1% 的門檻是 0.140721 和 0.404789。接著我把攻擊評估改成 A/B/B_adv 的 verification protocol。PGD 在五個 development identity 上都能讓 ArcFace 跨過門檻，但轉到 FaceNet 是 0 個。加入 MI-FGSM 後，source model 的效果沒有 PGD 強，但 FaceNet 的變化更大，第一次看到 1 個跨過 FaceNet 門檻。這些都還是 development observation；held-out 的 100 identities 尚未使用，會等方法確定後才正式評估。

If professor asks about 0.347

舊的 0.347 和新的 B–B_adv 都是 self-similarity，所以可以直接比較。audit 顯示 PGD 的數學和 evaluator 沒有因 refactor 改變；主要差別是 input condition：舊的是 raw single image，現在是不同的 LFW funneled samples。現在只能說 attackability 有 sample/dataset dependency，不能指定是 funneling 造成。最重要的是，0.347 是 historical self-similarity，不是新的 A–B_adv verification metric，所以不能拿它當正式 verification success 或 failure。
